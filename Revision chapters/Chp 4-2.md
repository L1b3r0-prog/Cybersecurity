## Purpose of differential privacy
The core tension is when datasets contain sensitive information that is also useful, the challenge is enabling desirable use while protecting the privacy of data subjects

Types of sensitive data
  - Census data
  - Health records
  - Social network activity
  - Telecommunication data

Legit use of data
  - Academic research
  - Informing policy
  - Market analysis
Still is required to protect privacy
