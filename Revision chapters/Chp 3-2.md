## General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
Made by the European Union (EU)

Purpose:
- Outline new set of enforceable and uniform requirements for protecting the personal data of individuals across the EU

GDPR protects personal data of individuals


### What is personal data 
- Any data or any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person directly or indirectly
- Includes a name, identification number, location data, online identifier or one or more factors specific to the physical, physiological, genetic, mental, economic, cultural or social identity of that natural person
- Also applies to the processing of personal data, whether automated or not such as collecing, storing, organizing, etc.
- Doesn't apply to the processing of personal data

### Territorial scope
Based on two criteria
1. Establishment of controller or processor in the EU
2. Being active on the EU market by offering services or goods or monitoring behavior

Terms:
- Controller: Party who determines the purposes and means of the processing of personal data
- Processor: Party who processes personal data on behalf of the controller
- Person, public authority, agency or other body can be either one

### Key principles of GDPR
1. Lawfulness, fairness and transparency
2. Purpose limitation
3. Data minimization
4. Accuracy
5. Storage limitation
6. Integrity and Confidentiality
7. Accountability

First point:
- Lawfulness means that you don't do anything with the personal data in an unlawful sense, which includes statue and common law obligations
  - Lawful basis should be determined for the data processing activity
- Fairness means data subject must reasonably expect the processing and processing mustn't have unjustified adverse effects on them
- Transparency means being clear, open and honest with people from the start about who you are, how and why you use their personal info and their rights

Second point:
- The personal data shall be collected for specified, explicit and legit purposes and not further processed in a manner that is incompatible with those purposes.
- In practice
  - Be clear from outset why you are collecting personal data and what you intend to do with it
  - Comply with documentation obligations to specify purposes
  - Comply with your transparency obligations to inform individuals about purposes
  - Ensure that if you plan to use or disclose personal data for any purpose that is additional to or different from the originally specified purpose, the new use is fair, lawful and transparent

Third point:
- Personal data shall be adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary in relation to purpose for which they are processed
- Org should identify minimum amount of data that it needs to fulfill its purpose

Fourth point:
- Personal data must be
  - Accurate
  - Kept up to date
  - Erased or rectified without delay when inaccurate
- GDPR doesn't define the word "accurate"
- In practice
  - Take reasonable steps to ensure accuracy of personal data is clear
  - Ensure source and status of personal data is clear
  - Carefully consider any challenges to accuracy of information
  - Consider whether it is necessary to periodically update the information

Fifth point:
- Personal data shall be kept in a form which permits identification of data subjects for no longer than is necessary for the purposes for which the personal data are processed
- Personal data may be stored for longer periods solely for achieving purposes in the public interest, scientific or historical research purposes or statistical purposes
- Can't keep data longer than you actually need it

Sixth point:
- Must ensure that there is appropriate security measures in place to protect the personal data you hold
- Data must be processed in a manner that ensures appropriate security of personal data
  - Including protection against unauthorised or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction or damage
  - Using appropriate technical or organisational measure
- Egs. of technical measure
  - 2FA
- Egs. of organisational measure
  - Staff training or security policy

Seventh point:
- Controller shall be responsible for and be able to demonstrate compliance with all other principles mentioned above
- Controller must have appropriate measures and records in place to demonstrate compliance
- May include
  - Adopting policy and procedure
  - Taking 'data protection by design and default' approach
  - Implementing appropriate security measures
- Accountability obligations are on-going. Data controllers must review and where necessary, update measure that are in-place

## Australian Privacy Act 1988
It is the principal piece of Australian legislation protecting the handling of personal information about individuals which includes the collection, use, storage and disclosure of personal information in the federal public sector and in the private sector

### 13 Australian privacy principals
It governs standards, rights and obligations around
- Collection, use and disclosure of personal information
- Organisation or agency's governance and accountability
- Integrity and correction of perosnal information
- Right of individuals to access their personal information

### Part 1: Consideration of personal information privacy
APP1: Open and transparent management of personal information
- Objective is to ensure that APP entities manage personal information in an open and transparent way
- APP entity must have clearly expressed and up to date policy about management of personal information by the entity

APP2: anonymity and pseudonymity
- Individuals must have the option of not identifying themselves or of using a pseudonym when dealing with an APP entity in relation to a particular matter

### Part 2: Collection of personal information
APP3: Collection of solicited personal information
- Must only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary and must do so by lawful and fair means

APP4: Dealing with unsolicited personal information
- Individuals must have option of not identifying themselves or of usign a pseudonym when dealing with APP entity in relation to a particular matter

APP5: Notification of the collection of personal information
- Entity that collects information about individual must notify and ensure that the individual is aware of it

### Part 3: Dealing with personal information
APP 6: Use or disclosure of personal information
- Specify how the entity can use or disclose collected personal information

APP 7: Direct marketing
- Collected personal information must not be used for direct marketing except for some special cases

APP 8: Cross-border disclosure of personal information
- Outlines the requirements for disclosing personal information to other countries

APP 9: Adoption, use or disclosure of government related identifiers
- Specify the conditions of how the government related identifiers can be adopted, used and disclosed

### Part 4: Integrity of personal information
App 10: Quality of personal information
- Entity must ensure the quality (accuracy, completeness and timeliness) of data in both collection and usage or disclosure phases

APP 11: Security of personal information
- Entity must take steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss or unauthorized access
- Entity must take steps to destroy needless information to ensure that the information is de-identified

### Part 5: Access to and correction of personal information
APP 12: Access to personal information
- Ensures that individual can access his/her personal information stored on entity's side except with some special circumstances

APP 13: Correction of personal information
- Outlines the steps entity should do to correct the personal information
