## General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
Made by the European Union (EU)

Purpose:
- Outline new set of enforceable and uniform requirements for protecting the personal data of individuals across the EU

GDPR protects personal data of individuals


### What is personal data 
- Any data or any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person directly or indirectly
- Includes a name, identification number, location data, online identifier or one or more factors specific to the physical, physiological, genetic, mental, economic, cultural or social identity of that natural person
- Also applies to the processing of personal data, whether automated or not such as collecing, storing, organizing, etc.
- Doesn't apply to the processing of personal data

### Territorial scope
Based on two criteria
1. Establishment of controller or processor in the EU
2. Being active on the EU market by offering services or goods or monitoring behavior

Terms:
- Controller: Party who determines the purposes and means of the processing of personal data
- Processor: Party who processes personal data on behalf of the controller
- Person, public authority, agency or other body can be either one

### Key principles of GDPR
1. Lawfulness, fairness and transparency
2. Purpose limitation
3. Data minimization
4. Accuracy
5. Storage limitation
6. Integrity and Confidentiality
7. Accountability
